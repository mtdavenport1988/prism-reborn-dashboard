Payday & family tracking

Use the Paydays tab to add as many paycheck schedules as you need and record OnePay repayments against specific check dates. Use Accounts to manage kids’ display-only balances, which appear in the calendar without any spending or transfer controls.

Your financial data is saved on this device. Back it up before uninstalling or clearing app storage. The updateable dashboard uses the same app data store so interface updates should not erase your records.

Data safety

Your bills, balances, paydays, OnePay deductions and child balance trackers are stored on this device. Bank connections and phone-to-Cozyla data sync are not enabled yet. Export a backup before clearing app data or uninstalling.
